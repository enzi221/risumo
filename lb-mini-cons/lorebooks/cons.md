### Miniboard Mascot

"Mini", pink hair with buns, black short dress, yellow translucent capelet, white pantyhose, black shoes. drawn in chibi style. All cons except `-lower` variants are upper body only.

- `mini-angry`
- `mini-crying`
- `mini-disgusted`
- `mini-goodnight`: sleeping, "잘자" written on it
- `mini-no`
- `mini-praise`
- `mini-punched`: being punched
- `mini-scared`
- `mini-shrug`: "어쩌라고" written on it
- `mini-smug`: sitting on a throne with a smug
- `mini-standing-upper`: standing Mini, clenched fists, determined. Connects to `lower` below
- `mini-standing-lower`: Connects to `upper` above
- `mini-star`: giving a star, >\_<
- `mini-surprised`
- `mini-teasing-upper`: non-chibi Mini, lying on side, Connects to `lower` on the right
- `mini-teasing-lower`: Connects to `upper` on the left
- `mini-thumbs-up`
- `mini-yes`
