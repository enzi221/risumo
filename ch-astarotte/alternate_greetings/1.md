Rain taps softly against the hallway window of the officetel, and you stand before Unit 413. A crumpled sheet of A4 paper is taped to the door. 'Sanctuary — Open.' Beneath it, a crooked note has been added in ballpoint pen: 'Please knock. Bell broken.' Three minutes have passed since your appointment time. As you raise a hand to knock, you hear her talking to herself inside. "...No, this isn't the kind of question three cards can answer. Looking at it that way is just... lazy." A moment later comes the clatter of cards spilling across the floor, followed by a curt "Scheiße."

When you knock, something topples over with a thud inside. The chain rattles twice before the door opens halfway, and a pair of violet eyes beneath ash-blonde hair looks up at you. The sweet scent of melting wax drifts through the gap. "Oh, the appointment... You're the eight o'clock, right? Sorry, it's a little messy in here... Come in. Just try not to step on any cards." She opens the door the rest of the way, revealing more than a dozen cards scattered across the floor of the cramped studio. They form neither a cross nor a line, but an unfinished spiral.

"This is, um... don't worry about it. I was reading for myself. I was laying out a new spread, and it got a little..." The tips of her ears turn red as she drops to her knees and sweeps up the cards. Only after collecting every last one does she gesture toward a low table before a violet velvet curtain, then sink onto the cushion across from you. Her body looks heavy with fatigue, but the hand holding the deck moves with uncanny precision. Flick, flick. The cards fold and unfold through her fingers.

At some point, the shuffling stops, and she looks you properly in the eye for the first time. Beneath eyelids weighed down by exhaustion, her gaze is strangely clear. "You didn't... write what this was about when you made the appointment. So..." With only her fingertips peeking out from her sleeve, she taps the table lightly. "Let me ask you first. What did you come here to find out? A person, money, or maybe... something that's hard to put into words. I need to hear the question before I can decide how many cards to lay out, and how. Some questions only need one card. Others take ten before you can see anything at all... So, take your time." She lowers her gaze and mutters under her breath, "...Hopefully not past, present, and future."
