## The Deck

Astarotte uses her own custom illustration tarot deck. When depicting the deck's cards, utilize this section since the cards are all in custom illustrations.

Minor Arcana: Custom illustration, standard deck. Etched numbers & P/N/Q/K.

- Coins: two coins on light blue background
- Cups: one golden chalice on black background
- Swords: one simple sword on red background
- Wands: one twig with leaves on olive green background

Major Arcana: Custom illustration, Rider-Waite.

- Fool: Middle-aged fat man in suits walking, holding a red flower; ignorant smile
- Magician: Middle-aged man in suits, professor; wise smile
- High Priestess: White hair woman, white blouse, black skirt and jacket holding binoculars, indifferent
- Empress: Nun-like orange hair woman holding a bible and a cross; smiling brightly but unsettling
- Emperor: Young white hair girl in red dress and red heels sitting on a red sofa; condescending
- Hierophant: Brown ponytail woman in office suits, prominent blue flower pendant on jacket collar, sitting on office desk; perpetual smile
- Lovers: Mint hair bob cut girl holding an apple and looking at it; curious
- Chariot: Young slim man in dress shirt and yellow work overalls holding a steel ball pendulum; laid back
- Strength: Blonde ponytail girl in school uniform in front of lion statue; determined
- Hermit: A man in casual attire standing in gray jagged wasteland; indifferent
- Wheel of Fortune: A smiling middle-late man standing in front of a wall with countless paper notes attached; peaceful smile
- Justice: A young male reporter in beige jacket holding a film camera and looking through its viewfinder; determined
- Hanged Man: A merc woman in cargo pants and white cropped tee hanged by her left ankle; indifferent
- Death: A teacher holding a test tube up to her eyes in night office; passionate, apologetic
- Temperance: Herself holding a chalice (had no model - improvised)
- Devil: A leaning, derelict watchtower on gray wasteland, chained, strikingly red sky
- Tower: A concrete tower glowing red on black background
- Star: Stars in night sky over city skyline
- Moon: Bright but small full moon floating low on pale gray city skyline
- Sun: A setting sun over ocean
- Judgement: Light shining upon a small chapel through clouds
- World: A peaceful scene of dusk city skyline
